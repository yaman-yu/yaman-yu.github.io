Research

My research brings human factors together with system design to build human-centered safety, security, and privacy mechanisms for emerging technologies. Using empirical methods, system building, and design research, I study how risks emerge in real-world use and how technical systems can better protect the people who rely on them.

AI safety for youth and families

Risk taxonomies, safety benchmarks, and safeguard models for developmentally appropriate AI. YAIR (SOUPS 2025) · YouthSafe (CCS 2025) · Parent-Child Perceptions (IEEE S&P 2025) · Safe AI Companions (CHI 2026)

Usable security and privacy for at-risk users

Human-centered mechanisms that help people with different abilities and backgrounds make better privacy and security decisions. Inclusive Email Security (IEEE S&P 2023) · GuardLens (SOUPS 2023) · GenAI for Accessibility (ASSETS 2025)

Security and privacy in decentralized systems

How Web3 and crypto systems shift safety, security, and trust responsibilities onto individual users. Crypto Wallets (CHI 2024) · Stablecoin Security (IEEE S&P 2025) · Web3 Social Media (CHI 2025)
